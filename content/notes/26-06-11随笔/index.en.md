Spent quite a bit of time over the past few days consulting various classmates and finally got the whole business of finding an advisor and joining a research group sorted out.

Maybe it's because I felt like life had been too unproductive for a while and I wanted to do something about it.

Either way, I now have a rough plan for my future.

I'll probably try to publish a few papers during undergrad, aim for guaranteed postgrad admission somewhere decent, and figure out the further future when it comes.

The overall direction is settled — AI. The advisor I've chosen works in AI infra at a neighboring university.

Doing horizontal projects to chase funding isn't something I'm interested in; a small research group suits where I'm at right now.

Maybe the specific sub-direction doesn't matter that much. What matters more is the advisor, along with luck and fate — so I went with an advisor recommended by a friend who has a solid reputation.

Once the exams are over and I've moved to the Shenzhen campus, it'll be time to get busy. Hope everything goes smoothly.

Attaching the email I wrote to the advisor and my CV (with sensitive information redacted) — a little memento of this past few days' work.
