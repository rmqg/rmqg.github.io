Just writing whatever comes to mind.

Spent the whole day watching videos about what love is — came away with something, I think. Maybe a preliminary understanding.

Love is a kind of responsibility — growing together with the one you love, moving forward together.

Love is also a way of completing the self; what a person seeks is precisely what they lack within themselves.

Love is also a primal passion — one of the most beautiful qualities of being human, I suppose.

There are surely more thoughts I haven't managed to put into words. Words fall short, but I'll leave it like this for now.

Now that I've arrived at the Cybersecurity School, it's time to embrace a completely new beginning — and let that beginning start with the question of what love is. I'll probably read a bit more lately, pay a little more attention to metaphysical things, and then reflect on my own lived practice.

Maybe I'll grow a little more resolute, a little braver — and perhaps a little wiser too. I hope I can learn to love myself better, to love her, him, it, and to be loved in return.

No matter what, at least right now I still believe in hope, believe in miracles, believe in all love and beauty. I have courage, and I'll try to nurture my own confidence, try to better myself, and accept who I am.

Keep growing, young one — the poetry and the distance, the stars and the sea that belong to you are slowly unfolding.

Confusion, disorientation, pain, loneliness — all of it may be eternal, but always believe in the light.

I choose hope. "Don't refuse every beginning just because you're afraid of the end."

I like who I am.
